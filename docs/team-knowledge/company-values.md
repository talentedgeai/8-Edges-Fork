These are the beliefs that shape how Edge8 goes to market and builds, in ranked
order. (For how we work together day to day, see "How we work — team core
values".)

1. **Be tech-forward, not tech-curious.** Implementation over theory. Ship work;
   don't pitch frameworks.
2. **Global talent is a force multiplier.** Vietnamese AI talent paired with
   international founders is the wedge.
3. **Build, don't pitch.** Case studies are the credential. Word-of-mouth from
   real installs beats paid acquisition.
4. **Lineage matters.** Microsoft, TINYpulse, and Vinasource are the proof of
   operator credibility. Don't bury it.
5. **Calm.** One clear call to action. No funnel jujitsu. The work sells the work.
