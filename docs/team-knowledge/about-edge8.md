Edge8 is an AI consulting and global talent firm. We help founders and CEOs
install AI leadership, build AI programs, and staff their teams with
Vietnam-based, AI-trained talent.

Our point of view is simple: most AI consultants talk theory, Edge8 builds. The
company itself is the proof: a real operator track from Microsoft to Vinasource
to TINYpulse to Edge8, not a thought-leadership career.

Who we serve, in order of the question they walk in with:

- Founders and CEOs of mid-sized businesses: "Where do I actually start with AI
  in my company?"
- AI-trained talent (Vietnam-first) looking for international placement: "How do
  I get placed at an AI-first company?"
- Workshop and training prospects: "How do I become AI-literate as a leader?"
- Existing clients and the broader ecosystem: "Who else has done this, and what
  came of it?"
