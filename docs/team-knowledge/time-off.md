You request and track leave in the **Time Off** section of the portal
(/team/time-off).

- **Requesting leave:** open Time Off, pick the dates and the leave type, and
  submit. Your request goes to your manager for approval.
- **Your balance:** the Time Off page shows your leave policy and how many days
  you have used and have left. The assistant can tell you who is off and when,
  but for your own up-to-date balance the Time Off page is the source.
- **Approvals:** managers review and approve their team's requests. If you manage
  people, pending requests appear in your approvals.

Edge8 operates on Vietnam time (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). Public holidays follow the
Vietnam calendar.

For anything this doesn't cover (carry-over rules, unpaid leave, special
circumstances), check with your manager or People Ops.
