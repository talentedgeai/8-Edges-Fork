The assistant is the chat button in the corner of the Edge8 AI Workspace. Ask it
anything about the company in plain language.

**It can help with:**

- Your own details and colleagues: "who's my manager?", "who's in the design
  team and who do they report to?", "what's Mai's role?"
- The org and who's around: "who's out next week?", the reporting structure.
- How the company is doing: revenue, invoices, expenses, clients, and the sales
  pipeline — Edge8 is open-book with staff on the numbers.
- Company knowledge: values, how we work, time off, and other policies in this
  knowledge base.

**It can't:**

- Change anything. It only looks things up. To request leave or edit your
  profile, use the Time Off and My Profile sections.
- See payroll or individual compensation, sensitive personal data (bank details,
  government IDs, dates of birth), performance reviews or 1-1s, recruiting and
  candidate records, or survey responses. Those are deliberately off-limits.

If it can't answer something, it will say so. For anything sensitive or personal,
your manager or People Ops is the right place.
