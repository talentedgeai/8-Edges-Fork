# 8 Edges Operating System intro video, script v8

Target length: 2:55. Audience: CEO of a 100 to 500 person company.
Structure: two-gaps hook with Stanford proof, Four Offices intro as four outcomes, one segment per office. Every office segment ends on its outcome, spoken plainly. Written for the ear: short sentences, contractions, no framework vocabulary.

---

## 0:00–0:40 · The hook

**Visual:** a strategy deck on a boardroom screen. Cut to the same office weeks later: everyone busy, heads down in a dozen different tools. On the Stanford line, full-screen stat card: 77% · DATA + WORKFLOW REDESIGN · STANFORD DIGITAL ECONOMY LAB.

**VO:**
You run a company of two hundred people. You know the strategy. But do the other one hundred ninety nine actually know how to execute it?

And you want AI to help. Everyone does. But here's the reality: in most companies, AI is stuck. Nobody's designed the workflows, so it doesn't have a real job. And your data's spread across a dozen tools, so it has nothing solid to work from.

Don't take my word for it. Stanford looked at fifty one successful AI programs. 77% of the work was not the AI technology. It was the data, and redesigning the workflows.

## 0:40–1:05 · The Four Offices of the Future

**Visual:** four outcomes appear as words: greater revenue, higher performing talent, streamlined operations, innovative culture. Each resolves into its office in the Edge8 OS sidebar: Revenue, Talent, Operations, Innovation.

**VO:**
So where do you start? Not with a re-org.

Start with what you're actually trying to achieve. Four outcomes: greater revenue. Higher performing talent. Streamlined operations. And a culture that innovates.

We call this the Four Offices of the Future. It's a way to organize the work so AI can help, without reorganizing your business. One database underneath. Four offices on top. Let me show you each one, live.

## 1:05–1:33 · Office of Revenue

**Screen grabs:** the CRM company list (255 companies), the deal board mid-drag, one deal open with its full interaction history, then a call transcript dropped in and the branded proposal page appearing.

**VO:**
This is the Office of Revenue. Every contact, every company, every conversation, in one place.

Now watch this. A sales call ends. The transcript goes in. The AI updates the CRM, moves the deal, and drafts the proposal. Live, in under ten minutes. Your salesperson stays focused on the relationship, and nothing slips through the cracks.

And here's the finale. The AI coaches your salesperson on that call, as if Mark Roberge, the man who scaled HubSpot sales from zero to a hundred million, were right there in the room with them. What to ask next time, which objection to see coming, the move that closes it.

Deals move faster, and fewer of them die quietly. That's greater revenue.

## 1:33–2:00 · Office of Talent

**Screen grabs:** a job req with its stack-ranked applications (names blurred), one candidate open with AI score and summary beside the recruiter's own assessment, then the org chart and a 1-on-1 coaching prep page.

**VO:**
The Office of Talent. Three hundred applications just came in for your open roles. The AI has already read every single resume, scored every candidate, and ranked the list. Your recruiter meets the top five and makes the call.

And after the hire? Onboarding plans, the org chart, and every manager walking into their one-on-ones with a prepared brief instead of a blank page.

Your managers spend their time developing people, not pushing paper. That's higher performing talent.

## 2:00–2:22 · Office of Operations

**Screen grabs:** the time off board approving a request, the equipment register, a work request moving across the board, the finance sync pulling invoices from the accounting system.

**VO:**
The Office of Operations. Leave requests, equipment, vendors, the books. All the machinery that quietly eats your managers' week.

Operations is usually seen as a cost center. It is one. Which means it has to run efficiently, not just to save you money, but to save your team the hours they should be spending on innovation and revenue.

Here, the AI does the tracking and the syncing, straight from the source systems. Your people just approve and decide. No chasing, no stale spreadsheets, nothing waiting in an inbox. That's streamlined operations.

## 2:22–2:45 · Office of Innovation

**Screen grabs:** the ideas feed with team submissions, the AI programs view, the workflows library.

**VO:**
And the Office of Innovation, the one most companies leave behind. It's the nice-to-have you pass on when you're already buried in operational busywork. But here's the catch: in the AI era, if you don't innovate, you're already falling behind someone who is.

Every idea from your team lands here, instead of dying in a chat thread. The best ones get built into new workflows, and every workflow you've seen today started exactly this way.

When your whole team is improving how the company runs, week after week? That's an innovative culture.

## 2:45–2:55 · Close

**Visual:** pull back to the full sidebar: four offices, one database. End card: 8 EDGES.

**VO:**
Four offices. One database. AI does the reading, the tracking, and the preparing. Your people do the deciding.

Greater revenue. Higher performing talent. Streamlined operations. A culture that innovates. We run our own company on this, and everything you just saw is live. This is 8 Edges.
