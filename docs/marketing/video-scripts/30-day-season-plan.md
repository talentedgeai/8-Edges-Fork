# 8 Edges video season: 30 days, one company, rebuilt in the open

The story: the CEO of a 200-person company knows the strategy, but the other 199 don't know how to execute it, and AI is handcuffed by two gaps: scattered data and undesigned workflows. Over 30 days we close both, workflow by workflow, until the company runs differently.

Structure: transformation arc as the spine (Act 1 diagnose, Act 2 build, Act 3 operate), pain-first episodes as the style. Every episode stands alone on social; watched in order, they tell one story that mirrors how we actually onboard an AI program client: audit, foundation, workflows, rhythm.

## Episode formula (every day, 2 to 3 minutes)

1. **Pain-first cold open**: a wound the viewer recognizes, in one or two lines. No product for the first 20 seconds.
2. **One real framework**, named and cited. One per episode, never two.
3. **Humans + AI in 8 Edges**: the demo, always answering the same two questions: what did the AI just do, and what does your person still decide.
4. **Outcome chorus**: land on the outcome line. "That's greater revenue." "That's higher performing talent." "That's streamlined operations." "That's an innovative culture."

Recurring rules: 8 Edges appears on screen in every episode, including Act 1; the diagnosis episodes end with a glimpse of the cure, the build episodes live inside it. Real screens, real data, names blurred. Plain words, no jargon. "Edge8" exact casing, no em dashes. Numbers carry between episodes so the season itself is the proof.
