AI Program Plan v2 — The Leadership Coach in the Company OS

Owner: Dave Hajdu  ·  Date: 2026-08-10  ·  Framework: the 5 Ds of AI program planning (A01: Define, Discover, Design, Determine, Deploy)
Status: design approved, build starting  ·  Replaces: v1 (Lark as system of record + local routines). Lark stays as the messaging channel and transcript source; it retires as the system of record

The Leadership Coach v1 proved the workflow: AI preps every biweekly 1-1 to my method, logs every commitment, reads retention risk, and holds me to coaching instead of telling. v2 moves the whole system into the company OS: Supabase is the system of record, the Team portal is the interface, and the two-tier privacy model is enforced by code instead of discipline.

The rule above the whole plan, unchanged from v1: the coach preps and drafts, I decide. It never tells me to promote, manage out, rank, or label a person. It surfaces the signal and proposes the move. I coach the team.

1DDefine the problem

The v1 problem stands: I lead five people, and my 1-1s are only as good as my prep and my memory. v2 adds two problems v1 could not solve:

  • Privacy by access control, not discipline. In Lark, who sees a private coaching read vs. a shared recap was a matter of care. In the company OS, the private coach layer and the person-facing layer are separate columns with separate query scopes. Mixing them is a code bug, not a human slip.
  • A system any coach can run. The coaching relationship is explicit (coach_id, independent of manager_id, dotted lines supported). Today the roster is mine; the model already works for Mai coaching her team next.

Success looks like: every report has current FAST goals laddered to a company OKR or KPI; every 1-1 is prepped, held, and logged in the portal; retention risk is named early; my coach / mentor / direct split moves toward 80 / 15 / 5; Lark and the laptop routines are fully retired.

3DDesign the workflow

The concept model: goals vs. priorities vs. KPIs

ConceptHorizonWhat it isWho sees it
FAST Goals (1 or more)Quarter What the person is driving this quarter. Frequent, Ambitious, Specific, Transparent. Each goal optionally ladders to an objective, a key result, or a KPI metric — at whichever altitude fits. The whole team. Transparent is the T in FAST.
PrioritiesWeek to week Standing focus items we look at in every 1-1 (P1, P2...). The working agenda between goals and the day-to-day. Coach + member.
KPIsContinuous Numbers the person owns (days-to-hire ≤ 20, monthly forecast accuracy). A metrics row with target, direction, and readings over time — so a goal or priority linked to one shows live progress automatically. Coach + member (rollups visible where the metric is used).
CommitmentsCycle to cycle What was agreed in the 1-1, who owns it, by when. Always "commitments," never tasks. Coach + member; member updates status + note.

The OCEAN profile

  • Structured: five dimensions, each with a rating and behavioral evidence, plus a personality snapshot and growth guidance.
  • Coach-authored, member-readable. Each person sees their own full profile, including the guidance, rewritten in second person so it reads as growth coaching. No comments in the app; the profile is a standing 1-1 topic.
  • Reviewed by me before any profile goes member-visible.

4DDetermine the ROI

  • Leverage. Roughly an hour of prep + recap per person per cycle, about 5 hours a cycle, ~130 hours a year handed back, against a small per-cycle token cost.
  • Retention. The coach reads the three embeddedness roots (fit, links, sacrifice) and names the thinnest one early. Keeping one senior person who would otherwise drift out pays for the build many times over.
  • Development that ladders. Every FAST goal ties to a 2026 KR or KPI. Coaching that moves FAST goals moves company KRs — and O2-KR1 ("100% of the team has a FAST goal, reviewed each cycle") is met by this system existing.
  • My own growth, measured. Mode split per 1-1, logged in the database: baseline 15/45/40 → last Lark cycle 60/20/20 → target 80/15/5. Now trendable in SQL.
  • New in v2: zero laptop dependency (cron runs in the cloud whether or not my Mac is open), enforced privacy, and a coaching system the next coach inherits for free.

5DDeploy to production

Runtime

  • System of record: Supabase company_os (six coaching tables live, three additions in this build).
  • Interface: the Team portal. Coach at /team/coaching, member at /team/my-coaching, goals transparent on team profiles.
  • Scheduler: daily Vercel cron at 07:45 Saigon — prep nudges, overdue-1-1 flags, mid-cycle check-ins, monthly trends, Minutes auto-detection. Every nudge goes out as a Lark DM and a Resend email.
  • AI: Claude Opus for prep, recap, and trends (coaching nuance justifies the tier). Every call fail-soft; errors land on the row, never block the workflow.

Guardrails, always on

  • The coach preps and drafts; I decide. Never a promote / manage-out / rank / label recommendation.
  • Private and person-facing never mix; the shared recap publishes only after my review.
  • No permanent people decisions during an emotional spike.
  • Every claim points to a behavior in the data. Thin reads say low-confidence. TBD is never fabricated.
