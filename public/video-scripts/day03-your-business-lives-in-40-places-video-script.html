The forty places

Production
Dave to camera
Rapid B-roll as each is named: a CRM tab, a spreadsheet, an inbox, an accounting app, a chat thread
Counter graphic ticking up: 5... 12... 27... 40 TOOLS
CUT POINT

Quick audit. Where does your business actually live?

Customers in the CRM. Except the newest ones, they're in someone's inbox. Pricing in a spreadsheet. The real pricing in a different spreadsheet. People data in an HR tool, the books in accounting software, decisions in chat threads nobody can search.

Most companies run on about forty disconnected places. And then they ask AI to help. With what? AI can only create value where it can see the data.

0:45

The 6 percent

Production
Full-screen stat card: ONLY 6% OF COMPANIES HAD AI-READY DATA · STANFORD
Second card: IF IT'S NOT IN THE SYSTEM, IT DIDN'T HAPPEN
CUT POINT

When Stanford studied enterprise AI deployments, they found only six percent of companies had data that was actually ready for AI. Six percent. That's the club that wins, and the entry fee is one decision: one home for every fact, and a rule your whole company lives by: if it's not in the system, it didn't happen.

1:20

One database in 8 Edges

Production
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/companies, let the full company list load (255 companies), scroll once, slowly
SCREEN RECORD: click ONE company open; walk its tabs in order: people, deals, meetings, invoices, all on the same record
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/deals to show the same deal appearing on the board
SCREEN RECORD: then the payoff: open the admin assistant chat on any /admin page and TYPE a real question, e.g. "which clients have open deals but no meeting in 30 days?" and let the answer render on camera
Blur any sensitive amounts in post if needed
CUT POINT

Here's what one home looks like. This is our company, live.

Every company we work with, on one screen. Open one, and everything is already there: the people, the deals, the meetings, the invoices. Not five tools stitched together. One record.

And because it's one database, watch what the AI can do. I just ask, in plain English: which clients have open deals but no meeting in the last thirty days? And it answers. Across all of it. Instantly. That question used to take three people and two days.
