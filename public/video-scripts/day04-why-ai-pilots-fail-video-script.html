You bought the tools. Nothing changed.

Production
Dave to camera
B-roll: a stack of AI product logos / license invoices
Beat of silence after "nothing changed"
CUT POINT

Last year you bought the AI tools. Copilots, subscriptions, a pilot project with a nice deck.

Be honest: what changed? For most companies, the answer is nothing. The pilots demo well, and then they die quietly, and everyone concludes AI isn't ready.

AI is ready. The company isn't. And there's hard data on exactly why.

0:40

The 77 percent, unpacked

Production
Full-screen stat card: 77% · DATA + WORKFLOW REDESIGN · STANFORD DIGITAL ECONOMY LAB
Card builds: 51 DEPLOYMENTS · 41 ORGANIZATIONS · THE WINNERS, NOT THE FAILURES
CUT POINT

Stanford studied fifty one successful AI deployments. Not the failures, the winners. And in seventy seven percent of them, the hard work was not the AI technology. It was the data, and redesigning the workflows.

Think about what that means. The companies that win at AI spend most of their effort on two unglamorous things: getting the data into one place, and writing down who does what. A workflow is designed when every step names its owner: a person, or an AI.

1:30

A designed workflow, end to end

Production
SCREEN RECORD: this is the money demo, record it as ONE continuous take:
SCREEN RECORD: 1. open /admin/revenue/deals, show the deal sitting in its current stage
SCREEN RECORD: 2. open /admin/revenue/meetings and paste/upload a sales call transcript
SCREEN RECORD: 3. show the CRM record updating: notes attached, deal stage moved
SCREEN RECORD: 4. open the generated proposal page on edge8.ai/proposals/ in a new tab, scroll it top to bottom
SCREEN RECORD: 5. cut back to the deal board: the deal now in the proposal stage
On-screen timer running in the corner from step 2 to step 4: under 10 minutes
CUT POINT

So let me show you one designed workflow, start to finish, in our company.

A sales call just ended. The transcript goes into the system. That's the last thing a human types.

The AI reads the call, updates the client record, moves the deal, and drafts the proposal. And there it is: a live proposal page, on our domain, ready to send. Under ten minutes from hanging up the phone.

Every step of that workflow has an owner. The human owns the call and the price. The AI owns everything in between. That's what the seventy seven percent buys you.
