Nobody wrote down who does what

Production
Dave to camera, tight single, warm light, no music
B-roll: two people at a whiteboard shrugging; a hallway conversation
Land "the busiest week decides what slips" to camera
CUT POINT

Ask anyone in your company how a sales call becomes a proposal. Or how a new hire gets their laptop. You'll get a shrug, or a story.

The process lives in people's heads. Nobody wrote down who does what. So every workflow runs on memory and goodwill, and the busiest week decides what slips.

And when something does slip, you can't even ask why. There was no design to compare it to. Just a vague sense that someone should have done something.

0:40

The playbook, written twice

Production
Card: THE PLAYBOOK · HUMAN-READABLE + AGENT-EXECUTABLE
Card builds: EVERY STEP HAS A NAME · A PERSON OR AN AGENT · NEVER "THE TEAM"
Land "humans decide, the AI produces, and the design says which is which" to camera
CUT POINT

The framework is the playbook, and it gets written twice. Human-readable, so your people know exactly which steps are theirs. And agent-executable, so the AI knows exactly which steps are its.

Every step carries a name. A person, or an agent. Never "the team". When a human owns a step, the playbook says what they decide. When the AI owns a step, the playbook says what it produces. Humans decide, the AI produces, and the design says which is which. That split is the whole anatomy.

Most companies skip this because it feels like paperwork. It's the opposite. One page per workflow is enough. And that page is what lets you hand half the work to software without losing control of any of it.

One real playbook in 8 Edges

Production
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/deals, pan slowly across the board columns left to right, names blurred
SCREEN RECORD: click one deal card open and hold on the owner's name for two seconds
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/meetings, click one meeting open, scroll to show a call transcript attached to a client
SCREEN RECORD: open a public proposal page at edge8.ai/proposals/ and scroll it slowly top to bottom
Cursor moves slowly; every click deliberate; no fast scrolling
CUT POINT

Here's one real playbook in 8 Edges: a sales call becoming a proposal.

The human owns the call, the relationship, and the price. Then one administrative act: the transcript goes into the system. Everything after that line is the AI's job. It reads the call, updates the client record, moves the deal on this board, and drafts a proposal page on our own domain.

Notice what the human never touches anymore. No write-up. No formatting. No chasing. And notice what the AI never touches. The relationship. The price. The judgment.

Every step you just saw has exactly one name on it. On day twelve you'll watch this run end to end, against the clock. Today, just notice the anatomy: the AI reads and prepares, your people judge and decide, and none of it depends on anyone's memory.
