The warm buyer cools

Production
Dave to camera, tight single, warm light, no music
B-roll: a calendar filling up; a proposal doc sitting in drafts
Land "speed is the sale" to camera
CUT POINT

The call goes well. The buyer's warm.

Then the write-up waits behind other work, and the proposal waits behind that. A week later you send a document to someone who's already cooled. Nobody decided this. It's just what happens when a workflow has no design.

We lived this. Our proposals took a week, and the week was pure admin. And every day of delay is a day the buyer talks themselves out of it.

Speed isn't a nice-to-have in sales. Speed is the sale.

0:40

Engineer the process

Production
Card: MARK ROBERGE · THE SALES ACCELERATION FORMULA
Card: MEASURABLE · REPEATABLE · ENGINEERED
Land "everything after that is the AI's job" to camera
CUT POINT

Mark Roberge wrote the book on this: The Sales Acceleration Formula. His argument is simple. Sales stops being an art the moment you make it a process: measurable, repeatable, engineered.

He built HubSpot's sales machine that way, and the lesson transfers to a company of any size. Design the steps. Measure the steps. Then hand the right steps to software. Repeatable beats brilliant, because repeatable scales.

So we engineered our worst gap, call to proposal. The human owns the call, the relationship, and the price. Then one act: the transcript goes into the system. Everything after that is the AI's job.

The ten-minute run

Production
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/meetings, add the call transcript to the client's record, names blurred
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/companies, click the same client open, show the record updated from the call
SCREEN RECORD: open /admin/revenue/deals, show the deal moved to its new column on the board
SCREEN RECORD: open the drafted proposal page at edge8.ai/proposals/, scroll slowly top to bottom
Overlay: a running timer in the corner for the whole demo; end card freezes it under 10:00
Cursor moves slowly; every click deliberate; no fast scrolling
CUT POINT

Watch the real thing. The call just ended. I drop the transcript into the system, and that's my one admin act of the day.

The AI reads the call. It updates the client record. It moves the deal on the board. And it drafts a proposal page on our own domain, in our own format, ready to review.

I check it, and it's live. Under ten minutes from hanging up the phone. It was ten minutes the first time we ran it, and it's been ten minutes ever since, because software doesn't have a busy week. And no step depended on my memory.

Nothing you just watched needed a prompt. The playbook from day nine runs the whole thing. And the proposal can land while the call is still fresh in the buyer's mind, sometimes the same hour.
