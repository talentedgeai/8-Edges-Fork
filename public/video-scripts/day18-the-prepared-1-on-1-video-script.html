Walking in blank

Production
Dave to camera, tight single, warm light, no music
B-roll: a manager scrolling chat two minutes before a meeting, calendar reminder popping
Land "blank is a message" to camera, hold
CUT POINT

Every two weeks, your manager sits down with each person on the team. And walks in blank.

Two minutes before the meeting, they're scrolling chat, trying to remember what was agreed last time. So the meeting becomes a status update. What are you working on. Cool. Same time in two weeks.

The single best development tool a manager has, and it runs on improvisation. Your people notice. Blank is a message, and it says: this meeting matters less to you than it does to me.

0:40

GROW: four questions, in order

Production
Card: SIR JOHN WHITMORE · COACHING FOR PERFORMANCE
Card builds: GROW · GOAL · REALITY · OPTIONS · WILL
Land "so we stopped asking them to" to camera
CUT POINT

Sir John Whitmore gave coaching its most used structure: GROW. Goal, Reality, Options, Will. What are we trying to reach. Where are things really. What could we try. What will you actually do.

Four questions, in that order, and a status update becomes a coaching conversation. Most managers know GROW exists. Almost none can hold the structure at four on a Thursday, in meeting six of the day. So we stopped asking them to.

The prepared brief

Production
SCREEN RECORD: log into Edge8 OS, open /team/coaching; blur candidate and staff names
SCREEN RECORD: show the list of upcoming 1-on-1s, then click ONE session open
SCREEN RECORD: scroll the AI-prepared brief slowly, pausing on each GROW section heading in turn
SCREEN RECORD: scroll to the previous session's summary so the continuity from last time is visible
SCREEN RECORD: point the cursor at the notes field where the manager writes after the session, without typing
Cursor moves slowly; every click deliberate; no fast scrolling
CUT POINT

This is the coaching space in 8 Edges. Before every one-on-one, the manager opens a prepared brief.

The AI has already done the reading: the goals, the check-ins, what was agreed at the last session. And it structures all of it with GROW. The goal we're working toward. The reality since last time. Options worth raising. And the commitment to ask about.

The manager reads it in two minutes and walks in prepared. The AI did the reading. The manager does the coaching. After the session, the notes go back in, so the next brief starts where this one ended.
